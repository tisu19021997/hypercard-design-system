Visual effects
A Mac Plus redrew the screen in stages, and HyperCard made that a feature. Every transition is a sequence of frames showing only pixels of the old card, the new card or a destination image. Nothing blends, nothing eases. Stills below are mid-effect frames from old card One to new card Two.

Grammar
name · very slowly | slowly | fast | very fast · to card | black | white | gray | inverse
Speeds very slow 2400 ms · slow 1200 · normal 600 · fast 300 · very fast 150, at 12 frames per second. Calibrated by eye to a Mac Plus; tunable.
Reduced motion Every effect becomes a cut when prefers-reduced-motion is set. No CSS transitions anywhere; popups leave by dissolve or wipe, never by fading.
