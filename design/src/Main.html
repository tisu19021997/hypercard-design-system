HyperCard Design System
Apple HyperCard on a 1-bit Macintosh, 1987–1998. Tokens, CSS, effects, patterns, and rules for people and agents. Repo: hypercard-design-system
512 × 342 card · Chicago 12 · Geneva 12 · two colors
Five rules
1
Two colors. #000 and #fff. Gray is a pattern or a dither mask, never a hex value, never opacity.
2
Whole pixels. Menu bar 20, title bar 19 + 1, button 20, OK 59, scroll bar 16, checkbox 12. Integers from Apple's Toolbox code.
3
Hard edges. 1 px black borders. Shadows are solid offsets: 1 px windows, 2 px shadow parts. No blur, no gradient, no anti-aliasing.
4
Two voices of type. Chicago 12 speaks for the system. Geneva 12 speaks for the user. Geneva 9 for small labels, Monaco 9 for scripts.
5
Stepped motion. Cards change with a visual effect drawn in discrete frames: dissolve, wipe, iris, checkerboard, venetian blinds, zoom. Nothing eases or fades.
